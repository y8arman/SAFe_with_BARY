🎫 How to play

Joining: scan the QR on the big screen or open the site, tap Board a Game, and enter the 4-letter code. Phones, tablets, and laptops all work. You can hop on even after the train has left.

Answering: after the 3-2-1 countdown, tap your answer. Faster is better: a correct answer scores between 500 points (at the buzzer) and 1000 points (instant).

Streaks: every consecutive correct answer beyond the first adds a +100 bonus, up to +500. A wrong or missed answer resets it.

The final stop: all points pay DOUBLE on the last question. Comebacks happen.

The finale: podium, medals, fireworks, and the full passenger manifest. May the best passenger win.
